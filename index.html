Questions sellers ask

Anything else? Message us on WhatsApp and we'll reply with details.

What is the minimum order quantity?

We keep the minimum order low so new sellers can start small. Share your details and we'll send current MOQ for each collection on WhatsApp.

Do you provide product photos for listing?

Yes. You get ready product photos you can upload directly to your listings.

Is GST mandatory to start?

Not to enquire or buy from us. Some platforms ask for GST when you register as a seller; we'll guide you on what your platform needs.

Which platforms do you help with onboarding?

Amazon, Flipkart, Meesho, Myntra, Ajio, Nykaa, JioMart, Shopify or your own website, Instagram, Facebook and WhatsApp Business.

Do you manage my seller account after onboarding?

If you want us to. We can handle listings, orders and day-to-day account work after your store goes live.

How fast is dispatch?

Orders are packed and dispatched from our Surat shop. Ask us on WhatsApp for current timelines to your city.

What is your return or damage policy?

Message us with photos of any damaged piece and our team will explain the replacement process for your order.
